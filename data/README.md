`SR2311_SR2408_eDNA_metadata.csv` is the sample metadata (field, filtration and extraction records) for the filters and libraries of cruises RS2311 and SR2408. It is read by `scripts/01_csvs_to_phyloseq.R`. The processed data (taxon tables, corrected metadata, phyloseq files) are in `processed_data/`.

**Raw sequence data.** The raw sequencing reads (FASTQ) are archived in NCBI SRA under BioProject PRJNA1556353.
